Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about preserving, savoring, and commissioning specialty imported confectionery.

How should I store artisanal imported chocolates?

Store in a cool, dry environment between 15°C and 18°C (59°F–64°F) with humidity below 50%. Avoid regular home refrigerators as condensation causes sugar bloom. A dedicated wine cooler is ideal.

What is the shelf life of fresh cream truffles?

Because authentic Swiss and Belgian fresh cream truffles contain no artificial preservatives, they should be enjoyed within 14 to 21 days of arrival. Solid dark chocolate bars remain optimal for 18 months.

Can you customize chocolate packaging with company logos?

Yes. We offer custom foil-stamped ribbons, engraved brass plaques, and personalized wax seals with company monograms for orders of 25 boxes or more.

Are your imported chocolates certified ethically traded?

Every chocolatier in our portfolio trades directly with smallholder farmers, paying premiums between 150% and 380% above standard Fairtrade minimums to support regenerative agriculture.
