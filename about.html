OUR JOURNEY

A Legacy Built on Passion & Craftsmanship

Cacao & Cru was conceived by a small consortium of French-Swiss chocolatiers and agronomists with a singular mission: to preserve extraordinary confectionery creations and rare cacao traditions.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

Preserving the Art of Extraordinary Chocolate

While mass-produced confections often compromise on ingredients and craftsmanship, we operate differently. We work directly with family-held ateliers and ancestral cacao estates.

Our approach preserves the delicate floral aromas, authentic flavours, and exceptional quality intended by master makers.

From carefully selected cacao beans to handcrafted European confectionery, every creation represents our commitment to authenticity and excellence.

Our Business Model

The Four Inviolable Tenets of Our Sourcing Standard

Every imported bar, praline ballotin, and pastry chef block in our inventory must satisfy all four rigorous criteria.

1. Unadulterated and Purity

100% pure cocoa butter. Zero palm oils, hydrogenated fats, artificial vanillin, or chemical soy emulsifiers ever enter our warehouse.

Zero Synthetics

2. Royal & Atelier Exclusives

Exclusive North American and Asian distribution allocations for historic European royal purveyors and micro-batch artisan masters.

Direct Allocation

3. Living Terroir Traceability

From Esmeraldas cloud forests to Ambanja valleys, each bar carries verified harvest vintage years, tree varietals, and fermentation curves.

Single Estate

4. 3.8x Living Wage Premium

We bypass commodity exchanges entirely, paying nearly four times Fairtrade minimums directly to heirloom cacao farmer families.

Regenerative Bounty
